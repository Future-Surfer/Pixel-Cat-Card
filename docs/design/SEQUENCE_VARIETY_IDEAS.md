# Sequence variety ideas

Owner request, 20 November 2026: as features develop, record useful new sequences and variants of existing clips. This is an idea intake, not approved artwork, an integration receipt or a runtime task list. [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) owns priorities. Check the existing catalogue and exact saved versions before drafting; reuse suitable poses and tracks where possible.

| Feature / date | Proposed variant | Visual purpose | Contact and transition needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Edge scratching · 20 Nov | A slower two-paw rhythm and a quicker short burst, with different leading paws | Vary the energy and cadence without changing the standing location | Keep hind feet planted and each active forepaw on the edge; preserve the current entry/exit anchors. Compare existing independent arm tracks before authoring. |
| Edge scratching · 20 Nov | Pause the claws briefly and glance sideways, then optionally resume | Give a sustained scratch an attentive quiet alternative | Keep support/contact fixed during the glance. Resume or leave independently; no required response from another cat. |
| Edge scratching · 20 Nov | A short single-paw test followed by a look or withdrawal | Add a small alternative to a full scratching bout | An explicit paw-touch event; a supported exit to stand/walk. Touching the edge is not automatically a completed long scratch. |
| Edge scratching · 20 Nov | Lift and shake one forepaw after scratching; optionally lick it | Add a small finishing accent usable at a post or card edge | Release the edge before the shake, keep the other supporting paws stable, and offer direct interruption to movement. Grooming remains optional. |

All four are **ideas only**. None requires a compulsory scratch → look → groom chain. Authored entry/cycle/exit, mirrored contacts, kitten/adult/senior fit, interruption and exact-version visual review precede any integration. New features should add concise rows here when a concrete visual opportunity appears, rather than generating a generic animation wish list.

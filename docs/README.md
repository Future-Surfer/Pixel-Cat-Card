## Maintenance

- Keep short entry documents and one current handoff. Detailed contracts belong in their reference; dated evidence belongs in history.
- State a fact in its owning document; link from summaries. Avoid copying implementation narratives/module lists across files.
- Keep historical text immutable in meaning, clearly dated and out of routine reading. Relative links in relocated snapshots were adjusted.
- Historical proposed YAML is not a supported API. Source artwork, saved-version hashes and completed receipt records remain in place.

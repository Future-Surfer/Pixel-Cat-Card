How contact guides work
Post anchor (orange): aligns the whole frame to the current grip position on the post. Moving it changes the pose placement.
Forepaw A / B (green): mark the two front paws. Put each guide on the intended visible paw, regardless of its near/far drawing order. Moving a guide does not repaint the paw or automatically move the cat.
Depth paint: Always near (solid cyan border) draws those existing pixels in front of the post; Always far (dashed violet border) draws them behind it. These apply in every phase and can mark a head, tail or paw. They do not move the cat or set a grip. Existing wrap pixels keep their near role.
Contact phase: says whether the pose grips, pushes or is released. Only released phases gain height. Rear paws currently have no separate tracked guides; A / B do not mean front / rear or near / far. Four named paw contacts are planned.
Unfinished forepaw guides warn during the lab preview; they do not prevent you from watching your edited animation.


Left-drag paints; right-drag temporarily erases without changing your selected brush. Cancelled pixel strokes leave the frame unchanged. Orange = post anchor; green = two forepaw guides. Small green squares mark contacts; solid cyan cell borders mean always near, dashed violet borders mean always far. Borders are editor-only and do not change the cat colour.
